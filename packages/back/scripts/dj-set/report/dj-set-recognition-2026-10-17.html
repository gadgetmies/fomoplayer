How a layer is placed, using Tarantula as the example

The renderer pins the layer's source cue (set_layer.src_cue_index, 0:49.9 into Tarantula) to the backbone cue plus offset (Super Sharp Shooter 2:21.0). It then plays the source beat-locked at the backbone's tempo, and the layer is heard only where its drawn volume lanes (volA × stemVoxA) are above zero. The pink bands are the three vocal phrases that play. The green box is the preview.

Checked against the extractor's own TrackStore.set_render_chain_spec, run on a copy of the catalog: all 8 layer windows match its compiled volume envelopes to 0.1 s, and every source and backbone cue matches the catalog.

What my earlier answer got wrong

Before

  • Used only the backbone cue for layers and ignored src_cue_index, then called the layer's native position "unreliable".
  • Treated the four one-second timeline rows as unknown and ended Klax at the start of its outgoing transition region (1474 s).
  • Ignored set_beat_jump.
  • Result: 12 plays scored as "unknown coverage", outside recall.

Now

  • Layers: the source cue is pinned to the backbone cue, the track is beat-locked, and it is audible where its volume lanes are non-zero.
  • Tracks: audible across the transition regions on both sides. Klax plays 0:34–2:48 of the track, 34 s of it inside the preview, and it is found.
  • Beat jumps on Mutationz, Atlantis, Universe and Turbulence are mapped back to the original track positions.
  • Detections at a "wrong" position turned out to be repeats of the same audio, not bad data.

Why S30's anchor error looks large

The median anchor error is 54.6 s, but each error is an exact multiple of 8 bars at 176 BPM (10.9 s): 10.8, 43.5, 65.7, 87.2, 109.3, 142 s. Some tracks overlap their preview only in a looped intro or outro. There, the strongest alignment is another repeat of the same loop, while the tempo is still exact. Where the matcher locks onto the instance that actually played, the error is under 1.5 s: Fred V, Aurora, Voodoo People, Jungle Music, Super Sharp Shooter and VIER. This is inference from the numbers. I haven't checked it loop by loop.
